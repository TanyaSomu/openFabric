Deterministic Conflict Resolution Policy

Mathematical specification of specificity scoring and precedence resolution

01

More Specific Beats General

A rule targeting a specific entity path overrides broader collection or wildcard permissions regardless of allow/deny effect.

transactions/txn-456 (20 pts) > transactions (10 pts) > * (0 pts)
02

DENY Beats ALLOW

When conflicting permissions exist at the exact same highest specificity score, DENY unconditionally triumphs.

DENY (10 pts) + ALLOW (10 pts) ➔ DENY
03

Collection-Write Inheritance

Patterns ending in /* grant write access to the immediate collection endpoint for REST item creation.

wallets/*/transactions/* (22 pts) allows POST .../transactions
04

Default Deny Posture

Zero matching permissions automatically evaluates to DENY for strict zero-trust boundary security.

No Matching Rule ➔ DENY
